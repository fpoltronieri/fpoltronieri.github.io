Filippo Poltronieri, PhD

Researcher, Department of Engineering, University of Ferrara, Italy

I am a Researcher (RTD-A) at the Department of Engineering of the University of Ferrara, where I will take up a tenure-track position (RTT) in January 2027. I received my Ph.D. in Engineering Science from the University of Ferrara in 2021 and hold the Italian National Scientific Qualification (ASN) as Associate Professor. I am a member of the Distributed Systems Research Group and deputy coordinator of the Big Data & Compute Continuum Lab.

My research focuses on AI-driven management of cloud-native applications across the Compute Continuum: reinforcement learning and multi-objective optimization for microservice orchestration, Digital Twins of Kubernetes applications (KubeTwin), and LLM-based and neurosymbolic approaches to intent-based management. I also work on adaptive communications and distributed learning at the tactical edge, in collaboration with the Florida Institute for Human & Machine Cognition (IHMC), where I was a visiting researcher in 2016–2017 and 2018, and as an official member of the NATO STO IST-223 Research Task Group.

I am Associate Editor of the IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management and of the Journal of Network and Systems Management (Springer Nature). Our work received the IEEE ComSoc CNOM Best Paper Award 2024 and Best Paper Awards at CNSM 2023, ICMCIS 2023, and IEEE/IFIP NOMS 2020.

I collaborate with Ghent University, IBM T.J. Watson Research Center, St. John’s University, the University of Bologna, and Politecnico di Torino.
